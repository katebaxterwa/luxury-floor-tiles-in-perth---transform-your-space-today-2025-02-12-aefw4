About Perth Luxury Tiles

Founded in 2005, Perth Luxury Tiles began with a vision to bring world-class flooring solutions to Western Australian homes. What started as a small family business has grown into Perth's premier destination for luxury floor tiles, serving thousands of satisfied customers across the metropolitan area.

Our mission is to transform spaces through exceptional quality, innovative design, and unparalleled service. We believe in sustainable practices, supporting local communities, and maintaining the highest standards of craftsmanship in everything we do. Our core values of integrity, excellence, and customer satisfaction guide every project we undertake.
